## Plan before leaving

Check current weather and hydrological warnings from IMGW and consult the local authority or site manager for access information. Use designated entrances and respect signs, fences and closures. Choose a daylight route with a clear exit and tell someone where you are going and when you plan to return.

## Keep away from the water

Search only from dry, publicly accessible ground, with room to move inland. Give surf, slippery structures, cliff edges and river mouths a wide berth. Turn back if waves narrow the route, visibility drops, or reaching an exit would require approaching the water. Carry a charged phone and dress for wind and cold.

## Leave suspicious objects in place

Avoid handling unknown debris, containers or objects that could be hazardous. Move away, warn others from a safe distance, and contact local authorities if there is immediate danger. Keep children and pets away from the object.

An official warning or closure takes precedence over any Amber Index reading or promising field observation. Choose another activity if you cannot establish that the walk is safe and permitted.
